# Sample Refund Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Policy owner:** Customer Support  
**Effective date:** To be determined

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how refund requests are assessed, when requests are ineligible under the standard business rules, and when human review is required.

## 2. Refund window

Customers should submit refund requests within **30 calendar days** of the purchase date. Requests outside this period are rejected under the standard policy unless an authorized exception or applicable law requires otherwise.

## 3. Final-sale items

Items designated as final sale are not eligible for refunds under the standard business policy. Applicable consumer-protection laws and authorized exceptions take precedence where required.

The system should verify final-sale status using trusted order or product records, not rely solely on customer-submitted information.

## 4. Refunds above $500

Requests for amounts **greater than $500** require human review before an eligibility decision or payment authorization. A request for exactly $500 does not trigger this amount threshold, although other rules may require review.

## 5. Reasons that may qualify

The following reasons may qualify for consideration:

- Damaged item
- Incorrect item received
- Item not received
- Item not as described

These reasons do not guarantee approval. A support agent should examine the available evidence and order details before deciding.

Change-of-mind and other requests must also be assessed against the applicable terms and any required legal protections.

## 6. Suspicious or conflicting requests

Requests containing potential indicators of duplicate refunds, conflicting order details, suspected abuse, or other inconsistencies must be escalated for human review.

Automated keyword checks are preliminary flags only. They do not establish fraud or wrongdoing.

## 7. Decision outcomes

- **Submitted:** The request has been recorded.
- **Needs review:** A human must assess the request.
- **Escalated:** Additional verification or specialized review is required.
- **Approved:** An authorized reviewer has approved the request.
- **Rejected:** The request has been rejected under the applicable rules.
- **Awaiting information:** More information is required.
- **Paid:** The refund payment has been completed.

Submission, eligibility, approval, and payment are separate stages.

## 8. Review records

Reviewers should document their decision, the reason for the decision, supporting evidence, and any customer communication. Access to review and approval functions must be restricted to authorized personnel.

## 9. Governance

The business should review this policy periodically and confirm that it complies with applicable consumer-protection, privacy, and payment requirements. Legal requirements override conflicting internal rules.
